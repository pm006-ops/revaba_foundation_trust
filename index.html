Trust Introduction

Revaba Charitable Foundation Trust is a non-profit organization dedicated to the holistic development of underprivileged communities. Our mission is to create a better tomorrow by empowering individuals through education, healthcare, food, women empowerment, and community development.

We believe in compassion, equality, and sustainable change. Together, we can build a stronger, healthier, and more educated society.

Our Activities

Driven by vision, mission, and action.

Vision

To build a society where every individual has equal opportunities to grow, learn, and lead a dignified life.

Mission

To empower communities through sustainable initiatives in education, healthcare, environment, women empowerment, and social welfare.

Health Initiative

Organizing health check-up camps, medical assistance and awareness programs for a healthier community.

Tree Plantation

We promote a greener tomorrow by planting trees and spreading awareness about environmental conservation.

Education Initiative

Providing quality education, books, school kits and digital learning support to underprivileged children.

Founder's Message

"​Every individual has a dream, but when that dream is connected with the happiness, dignity, and well-being of others, it becomes a mission. ​Revaba Charitable Foundation Trust is more than an organization – it is an emotion, a commitment, and a responsibility towards society. ​We live in a world where many still lack access to basic needs – education, healthcare, equal opportunities, and a safe environment. With this trust, we aim to be a helping hand for those who need it the most. ​Our work is not just about charity, it is about creating opportunities, spreading smiles, and building a stronger, compassionate, and self-reliant society. ​I believe that small acts of kindness, when done together, can create waves of change that last for generations. This journey is challenging, but with your support, trust, and belief, we can achieve our vision of a better tomorrow. ​I invite you to be a part of this mission. Let’s come together, join hands, and create a positive impact in the lives of many."

Gurupriy Satish
